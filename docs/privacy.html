Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

About Brújula

Brújula is an offline wheel-of-life app maintained by the Brújula project at github.com/anderrow/brujula. It is a personal reflection tool, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition, or replace professional advice.

Data on your device

Your area scores, written notes, evaluation titles and dates, history and language selection are stored in the app’s private SQLite database. Reminder frequency, time, selected day and notification language are stored in private Android preferences. New installations contain no personal reflections or sample scores.

No collection or sharing

Brújula does not transmit your reflections to the developer or another server. It has no account, ads, analytics, tracking SDKs, cloud sync or Internet permission. The developer cannot read or recover your reflections. The app does not access contacts, location, health sensors or Health Connect.

Optional notifications

Reminders are off by default. Notification permission is requested only when you enable a reminder. Notifications are scheduled on your device and contain an invitation to reflect, not your notes or scores. You can disable reminders in the app. Android battery settings can delay them.

Backups you choose

You may export or import a JSON backup through Android’s document picker. Exported backups contain your reflections and are not encrypted by Brújula. You choose the destination and whether to share the file. If you choose a cloud document provider, that provider handles the file under its own terms and privacy policy. No broad storage permission is requested. Automatic Android cloud backups and device transfers are excluded.

Retention and deletion

Data remains on your device until you remove an evaluation, use Settings → Clear data and start fresh, clear Android app storage, or uninstall the app. A full reset also disables reminders. Updates preserve your data. Copies you export are separate: delete them yourself from their destination, including any cloud copies. No server account exists to delete.

Privacy questions

Use github.com/anderrow/brujula/issues for privacy questions or bug reports. Issues are public: do not include scores, notes, backups or other private information. The repository host processes information you submit under its own privacy policy.

Download website

The separate download website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process normal web request information under its privacy policy. The website remembers your selected language in local browser storage and requests public release information from GitHub. It does not receive your app’s reflection data.
