Privacy policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

About Brújula

Brújula is an offline wheel-of-life app for Android and iPhone, maintained by the Brújula project at github.com/anderrow/brujula. It is a personal reflection tool, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition, or replace professional advice.

Data on your device

On Android, area scores, written notes, evaluation titles and dates, history and language selection are stored in the app’s private SQLite database. Reminder frequency, time, selected day and notification language are stored in private Android preferences. On iPhone, scores, notes, check-in history and reminder settings are stored in protected files inside the app. Those files use complete file protection and are excluded from automatic device backups. The iPhone app starts with the phone language and can retain a separate interface-language preference in local UserDefaults. That preference is separate from reflection backups and from the protected reflection files. New installations contain no personal reflections or sample scores.

No collection or sharing

Brújula does not transmit your reflections to the developer or another server. It has no account, ads, analytics, tracking SDKs or cloud sync. The Android app has no Internet permission. The iPhone app does not connect to the internet. The developer cannot read or recover your reflections. Neither app accesses contacts, location or health sensors. Android does not use Health Connect. iPhone does not use HealthKit.

Optional notifications

Reminders are off by default. Notification permission is requested only when you enable a reminder. Notifications are scheduled on your device and contain an invitation to reflect, not your notes or scores. You can disable reminders in the app. Android battery settings can delay them. On iPhone, Focus and the iPhone notification settings apply.

Backups you choose

You may export or import a JSON backup through the system file picker: Android’s document picker, or Files on iPhone. Exported backups contain your reflections and are not encrypted by Brújula. You choose the destination and whether to share the file. If you choose a cloud document provider, that provider handles the file under its own terms and privacy policy. No broad storage permission is requested. Automatic Android cloud backups and device transfers are excluded. Automatic iPhone backups of the app’s files are excluded.

Retention and deletion

Data remains on your device until you remove an evaluation, use Settings → Clear data and start fresh, clear the app’s storage, or uninstall the app. A full reset also disables reminders. Updates preserve your data. Copies you export are separate: delete them yourself from their destination, including any cloud copies. No server account exists to delete.

Privacy questions

Use github.com/anderrow/brujula/issues for privacy questions or bug reports. Issues are public: do not include scores, notes, backups or other private information. The repository host processes information you submit under its own privacy policy.

Download website

The separate download website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process normal web request information under its privacy policy. The website remembers your selected language in local browser storage and requests public release information from GitHub. It does not receive your app’s reflection data.
